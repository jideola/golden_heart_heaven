## Our commitment
Golden Heart Haven LLC serves people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, and we want our website to be usable by everyone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

## What we do
- Every page can be navigated by keyboard, with visible focus indicators and a skip link.
- Images include descriptive alternative text.
- Text and background colors are chosen for readable contrast.
- Motion is reduced for visitors who have requested it in their system settings.
- Content is available in English and Spanish.

## Tell us if something is not working
If you have difficulty using any part of this site, please call or email us using the details on the Contact page. Tell us the page and what happened, and we will work to fix it and to provide the information in another format.
