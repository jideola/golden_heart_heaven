## Information we collect
When you use the contact form we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number (optional), your relationship to the person seeking services, and your message. We also record the page language and the time of submission to help us respond appropriately and to prevent spam.

## How we use it
We use this information only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties for marketing.

## Health information
Please do not include medical records, diagnoses, or other protected health information in the contact form. If services are pursued, we will collect that information through secure intake procedures in accordance with applicable law.

## Cookies and analytics
This website does not use advertising cookies. If analytics are enabled in the future, this policy will be updated to describe them.

## Retention
Inquiries are retained for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, then deleted.

## Your choices
You may ask us to correct or delete your inquiry at any time by contacting us at the phone number or email listed on the Contact page.

## Contact
Questions about this policy may be directed to Golden Heart Haven LLC, Houston, Texas.
